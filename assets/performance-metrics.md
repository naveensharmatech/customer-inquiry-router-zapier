# Performance Metrics

The project showcase's reported figures and their limitations are documented in [Performance and ROI](../docs/PERFORMANCE.md). The reported values are approximately 98% lower manual triage time, a change from about 24 hours to about 5 minutes for initial response, and lead capture rising from 60% to 100%.

These are case-study figures, not measurements generated by this repository's tests or independently audited telemetry. Use the measurement definitions and reporting template in the linked guide to validate a deployment with its own timestamped email, Zapier, and CRM data.
