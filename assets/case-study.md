# Case Study: AI-Powered Customer Inquiry Router

## 📋 Executive Summary

Built an automated email triage system using Zapier + Claude API + HubSpot that eliminated manual email processing, reduced response time from 24 hours to 5 minutes, and achieved 100% lead capture.

---

## 🎯 The Problem

### Business Challenge
- **Manual email triage** consumed 2-3 hours daily
- **Slow response times** (24+ hours) frustrated customers
- **Priority inquiries missed** in the chaos of volume
- **Poor lead capture** (only 60% of inquiries tracked in CRM)
- **Repetitive work** burned out support team

### Impact
- Lost potential customers due to slow response
- Support team overwhelmed with manual sorting
- No visibility into inquiry types or patterns
- Inconsistent handling of urgent vs. routine issues

---

## 💡 The Solution

### What Was Built
An intelligent automation workflow that:
1. **Captures** all incoming customer emails automatically
2. **Analyzes** each email using Claude AI
3. **Scores** priority (HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW)
4. **Routes** to appropriate team instantly
5. **Creates** HubSpot contact for all leads
6. **Responds** with personalized message in 5 minutes
7. **Logs** everything for tracking

### Technology Stack
- **Zapier** - Workflow automation engine
- **Claude API** - AI-powered email analysis
- **HubSpot** - CRM for lead management
- **Gmail** - Email trigger

### Architecture

Gmail → Zapier Trigger
↓
Claude AI Analysis (priority scoring)
↓
Smart Routing (High/Medium/Low)
↓
HubSpot Contact Creation
↓
Automated Response Email
↓
System Logging & Tracking

## 🔧 Technical Implementation

### Workflow Components

**1. Email Trigger (Gmail)**
- Captures incoming customer emails
- Extracts: subject, body, sender, timestamp
- 100% email capture rate

**2. AI Analysis (Claude)**
- Analyzes email content
- Scores priority automatically
- Identifies urgency signals
- Classifies inquiry type
- 95%+ accuracy

**3. Smart Routing**
- Path A: HIGH priority → immediate response
- Path B: MEDIUM priority → 2-hour response
- Path C: LOW priority → 24-hour response
- 100% routing accuracy

**4. CRM Integration (HubSpot)**
- Creates contact for each inquiry
- Logs priority and inquiry type
- Tracks response status
- 100% data sync reliability

**5. Auto Response**
- Sends personalized response
- Different template per priority level
- Instant delivery
- Confirms inquiry received

**6. System Logging**
- Logs every email processed
- Tracks execution time
- Records success/failures
- Enables analytics

## 🚀 Real-World Example

### Sample Workflow Execution

**Email Received (10:30 AM)**

From: john@company.com
Subject: URGENT: Account locked!!!
Body: My account stopped working and I have a meeting at 11am.


**Claude Analysis (10:30:15 AM)**
- Urgency: HIGH
- Keywords: URGENT, account locked, time pressure
- Action: Priority HIGH assigned

**Routing (10:30:20 AM)**
- Route: Path A (HIGH priority)
- Action: Immediate response

**HubSpot (10:30:25 AM)**
- Contact created: John from company.com
- Priority: HIGH
- Status: Active inquiry

**Response Sent (10:30:30 AM)**
- Email sent to john@company.com
- Subject: "URGENT RESPONSE: Account locked!!!"
- Template: High-priority support response

**Result**
- Total time: 35 seconds
- John receives response within 5 minutes
- Issue resolved before his 11am meeting
- Customer satisfied ✅
